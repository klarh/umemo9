Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

Summary

umemo stores all data locally in your browser. We do not operate any servers, collect any analytics, or transmit any data unless you explicitly enable Google Drive sync.

Data storage

Your flashcard decks, review history, and settings are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync it or import cards from an external URL.

Importing from external sources

When you add an external source URL (e.g. a CSV or TSV file) to a deck, your browser fetches that URL directly. This means the server hosting the file will receive your IP address and standard HTTP request headers. umemo does not proxy these requests through any intermediary. The content of imported files is stored only in your browser's local storage.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect your Google account, umemo uses the Google Drive API to read and write files only in its own application folder. umemo does not access any other files in your Drive. Your Google OAuth token is stored locally in your browser and is never sent to any third-party server.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the home screen, which revokes the token and stops all sync activity.

Cookies and tracking

umemo does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technologies. No data is shared with third parties.

Third-party services

The only external service used is the Google Identity Services library (loaded from accounts.google.com), which is required for the optional Google Drive sync feature. Google's own privacy policy applies to that library: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data deletion

You can delete individual decks from the app, or clear all data by clearing your browser's site data for this origin. If you have synced data to Google Drive, you can delete the umemo folder from your Drive directly.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer.
